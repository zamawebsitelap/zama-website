## Background

Practice Togetherly is a Northern Virginia marketplace that brings vetted wellness instructors to people's homes, apartment buildings, and communities. Private yoga, personal training, sound baths, and group sessions, with the price on the page and the whole booking handled in one place.

## What we delivered

- **Positioning and messaging.** A positioning statement, master message, and three messaging pillars.
- **Website conversion audit.** A review of messaging, structure, and the booking flow, with prioritized fixes and homepage headline options.
- **Content system.** Five content pillars and 20 ready-to-publish captions.
- **Referral program.** A structure and full messaging set designed to reward repeat weekly clients.
- **Provider ambassador toolkit.** A system that turns instructors into a distributed marketing network.
- **B2B go-to-market.** A ranked target list across property management, residential communities, and community organizations, plus a one-pager, rate card, event kit, and outreach sequence.
- **PR.** Story angles, a media pitch, and outreach to local and wellness publications.
- **Photo and video production.** A creative brief, plus photography and video captured in-house.
- **Measurement.** A week-one baseline, a week-six results read, and a simple dashboard the founder can keep running.

## Beyond the brief

When Practice Togetherly hosted an event with a local apartment building, we came out to capture additional footage of the partnership in action. We also coached the founder on sales, including joining her on a live sales call.
