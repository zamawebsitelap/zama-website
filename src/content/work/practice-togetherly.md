## The business

Practice Togetherly is a Northern Virginia wellness marketplace connecting people with interviewed, insured instructors for private yoga, personal training, sound baths, and group sessions. Sessions can happen at home, in apartment buildings, or in the wider community. Prices are visible up front, and customers can book in one place.

The service was ready, but awareness was not. The founder was managing growth alone, with three possible audiences competing for attention: recurring individual clients, community and property events, and one-off occasions such as weddings. A six-week sprint needed to choose a focused path rather than spread effort across all three.

## The strategy

We made community and property events the entry point, then connected that discovery to recurring individual bookings. Before sending more people to the business, we audited the website and booking flow so the path from interest to booking was clear. The message centered on what made the marketplace easy to try: vetted instructors, transparent prices, and booking handled in one place.

## The work

![A Practice Togetherly instructor coaches a member through a strength exercise at a community event.](/images/IMG_8018.jpg)

- **Positioning and messaging:** a positioning statement, master message, and three messaging pillars.
- **Website conversion audit:** prioritized improvements to the messaging, site structure, and booking flow, with homepage headline options.
- **Content system:** five content pillars and 20 ready-to-publish captions.
- **Referral and provider programs:** a referral program for repeat clients and an ambassador toolkit to help instructors introduce the service.
- **Community and B2B outreach:** a ranked target list, one-pager, rate card, event kit, and outreach sequence for property managers, residential communities, and local organizations.
- **PR and creative:** story angles, a media pitch, local outreach, and a photo and video library captured in-house.
- **Measurement:** a week-one baseline, a week-six results read, and a simple dashboard for the founder to continue using.

## Early traction

At the first September community events, **three people booked after walking in cold, with no paid advertising**. A property partner also offered an unsolicited introduction across a regional property portfolio, creating a next step for broader community partnerships.

These early signals came from a focused path: meet potential customers through local events, make the offer and pricing easy to understand, and give interested people a straightforward way to book. The sprint also left the founder with reusable messaging, referral and provider programs, outreach materials, and a content system—not just a one-time campaign.

![A Practice Togetherly instructor welcomes attendees at an outdoor community event.](/images/IMG_7987.jpg)

## Beyond the brief

When Practice Togetherly hosted an event with a local apartment building, we came out to capture the partnership in action. We also coached the founder on sales, including joining her on a live sales call. The work extended beyond marketing deliverables to help the founder build confidence in asking for the business.
